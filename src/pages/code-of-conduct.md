SAIMI is a meeting place for the Swiss medical image analysis community. It should be a
place where everyone — students, early-career researchers, senior academics, clinicians
and industry participants alike — can present work, ask questions and disagree about
methods without being made unwelcome.

This code of conduct applies to all SAIMI editions, to every associated event and social
gathering, and to SAIMI's online spaces.

## Expected behaviour

- Treat everyone with respect. Assume good faith.
- Critique ideas, methods and results — not people.
- Be mindful that participants differ widely in seniority, background, first language and
  familiarity with the field. Questions that seem basic are welcome.
- Respect people's stated names and pronouns.
- Ask before photographing or filming individuals, and respect a refusal.
- Unpublished work presented at SAIMI is shared in confidence. Do not circulate slides,
  posters or results without the authors' permission.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment in any form, including unwelcome attention of a sexual nature, following or
  intimidation, and repeated unwanted contact.
- Demeaning, insulting or exclusionary comments, including those relating to gender,
  gender identity, sexual orientation, disability, physical appearance, body size, race,
  ethnicity, nationality, religion, age, or academic background or institution.
- Deliberate disruption of talks, poster sessions or discussions.
- Retaliation against anyone who raises a concern under this code.

## Reporting a concern

If you experience or witness behaviour that breaches this code, please tell a member of
the local organising committee — they are identified on the edition website and reachable
on the day — or contact the steering committee.

You may also raise a concern after the event. Reports are handled discreetly and shared
only with those who need to be involved in responding. We will not take action you have
not agreed to, except where we judge that someone is at risk.

## Consequences

The organising committee may take any action it considers appropriate, including a warning,
requiring someone to leave a session, removing them from the event without a refund of any
travel or other costs, or excluding them from future editions.

## Acknowledgement

This code of conduct draws on the codes of conduct of the MICCAI Society and of comparable
academic meetings.
